    About this map

    Each skull represents a source record of reported or suspected harm at an approximate location. It does not confirm deaths, current activity or guilt of people at a site. Multiple records can describe the same place.

    Evidence labels distinguish public distress reporting from sites documented or suspected by a source publisher. Crawler records remain awaiting SOS Relay review. An unmarked area does not establish safety.

    Belt and Road relationships require separate evidence. Neither geography nor nationality establishes a connection.

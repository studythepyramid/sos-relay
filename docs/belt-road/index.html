    About this map

    Skulls mark locations associated with public reports of coercion. They do not confirm deaths, present activity or guilt of everyone at a site.

    Locations may be approximate. Source dates and precision are shown per report. The initial collection is small; an unmarked area does not mean it is safe.

    Belt and Road relationships require separate evidence. Neither geography nor nationality establishes a connection.

    Records are collected from public reporting and checked before a static export. No user messages, private victim identities or original leaked communications are hosted here.

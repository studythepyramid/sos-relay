Before a real SOS is published

Everyone could see the exact location, message and timestamp, plus any contact details you choose to include. Publication would be immediate after the location check.

This tool does not hide your network connection. Your ISP or network observer can see that you connected unless you already use a suitable circumvention tool.

Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, which receives your network address and the map areas requested. This local test stores your location, message, timestamp and opted-in contact in genone.db. It does not store a sender IP or device ID. Public-host logging has not been verified.

Location check is not connected in this demo. The live service will use OpenStreetMap buildings and residential, commercial and industrial areas as approximate restrictions. Missing or incorrect data can cause mistakes; this is not proof of ownership or a legal guarantee.

    About this map

    A skull means the source classifies the report as documented. A red “?” means an unverified or suspected report. Neither symbol is an SOS Relay verdict. Reports without a map position remain in the list. A marker does not confirm deaths, current activity or guilt of people at a site. Multiple records can describe the same place.

    SOS Relay provides information and channels for SOS communication. It links public sources and does not act as a certifying authority. Please check the source information yourself. An unmarked area does not establish safety.

    Belt and Road relationships require separate evidence. Neither geography nor nationality establishes a connection.

    No private victim identities or original leaked communications are hosted here.

In June 1917, a minor incident occurred in China,
when Zhang Xun, along with Kang Youwei from the Hundred Days' Reform, led five thousand soldiers with pigtails into Beijing,
kneeling to ask the young Emperor Puyi to restore his position. At that time, Puyi was twelve years old,
and he also said: "I am too young and lack the talent and virtue to bear such a great responsibility..."
This event is known as the Zhang Xun Restoration, which lasted only twelve days before being driven out.
However, it provided the revolutionaries with more reasons to use force.

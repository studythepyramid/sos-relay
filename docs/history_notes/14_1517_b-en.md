The triangular social structure is a固化后的 "Qin system" in China after Emperor Qin Shi Huang, and it remains the same until 2024. As the saying goes, 天无二日，人无二主 (there can be no two suns in heaven or two rulers on earth), Chinese society is accustomed to having a single apex. In such a hierarchical society, the goal of climbing is to reach this sole apex; opportunities for expansion in other directions have been cut off by the existence of this apex. Therefore, the social structure can be summarized as the Eastern cultural hierarchy: the class system of ruler-minister, father-son, and husband-wife relationships.

Furthermore, if a ruler is not discreet, he will lose his ministers; if a minister is not discreet, he will lose his position; and if any matter is not handled discreetly, it can lead to failure. Mutual suspicion among people is inevitable in such a social structure, as there is only one apex and one direction provided by society. Therefore, stepping on more people to climb higher is the only way forward.

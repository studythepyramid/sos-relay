 educational assembly line, 
 Xinhai Revolution and the end of the Qing Dynasty, Sun Yat-sen and Yuan Shikai, Republic of China, Mainland and Taiwan,

1905年， 爱因斯坦 发表相对论，以简中的语气来说，
他在物理学史上树起了又一块巨大的里程碑。
但是按照他所在的文化环境来说，
他本人就是里程碑。这有一点微妙的区别，
文明的社会里每个人就是他自己。

In 1905, Einstein published his theory of relativity. In the tone of Simplified Chinese, 
he erected another giant milestone in the history of physics.
But according to the cultural context he was in,
he himself was a milestone. There is a subtle difference here,
in a civilized society, everyone is themselves.

探索到深奥学问的大脑有何不同之外？
解剖台上人们反复研究过人脑的奥秘，
在学者的大脑中还没有找到令人惊奇的与众不同之外， 
他们的脑袋和农民的脑袋在结构上是几乎一样的。

What sets the brains of those who delve into deep学问的大脑有何不同之处？
On the dissecting table, people have repeatedly studied the mysteries of the human brain,
yet in the scholars' brains, there is nothing particularly surprising or different.
Their skulls are structurally almost the same as those of farmers.

思想不是来自血缘和遗传。
家族传承可以勉强的让后人继承权力、财富，
家庭环境也可以让后人习得经验、技能，
然而很明显人们并不是从血液得到的思想。

Thoughts do not come from bloodline and genetics.
Family inheritance can勉强let successors inherit power and wealth,
and family environment can allow successors to learn experiences and skills,
but it is obvious that people do not get their thoughts from their blood.

思想从何而来？

Where do thoughts come from?

我们可以把思想分为两部分， 一部分是知识，
另一部分是每一个人面对知识时学习、记忆、理解和运用的独属个人的某些方法。

We can divide thoughts into two parts: one is knowledge,
and the other is personal methods that each person uses to learn, remember, understand, and apply that knowledge.

在二十世纪之初，
记录思想的各种工具仍然以纸与笔为代表，
书籍和印刷术则提供了工业化的内容复制能力。
或者说工业化的过程中思想传播的成本也在悄然的大幅度的下降，
就是在这样的背景中，仍然，
我们观察到阅读同类书籍和报刊的人们会得到全然不同的思想。

At the beginning of the 20th century,
various tools for recording thoughts were still represented by paper and pen,
books and printing provided industrialized content replication capabilities.
Or, during the process of industrialization, the cost of spreading ideas was quietly and significantly reduced.
In such a context, we still observed that people reading similar books and newspapers would have entirely different thoughts.

这很明显，但是此时并没有多少学者深入研究这一现象。
普鲁士式的教育要求学生们对着同一篇文章总结出同样的中心思想，
这正好满足了中国传统文化隐密的口味，
千年的伟大文化传承中不同朝代的学生们学了同样的内容爬向同样愚蠢的集权宝塔。

It is obvious, but at this time, few scholars had delved into studying this phenomenon.
The Prussian style of education required students to extract the same central idea from the same article,
which恰好满足了中国传统文化含蓄的口味，
在千年的伟大文化传承中，不同朝代的学生们学习相同的内容爬向同样愚蠢的集权宝塔。

从遗传学的进展来说，
现在人们不清楚慈禧太后是不是知道她已经把清朝的根基刨得差不多了，
仅剩的皇帝 光绪 藏在冷宫，
除此之外并没有一个年龄合适的太子来学习和继承满族统治中国的经验。

From the advancements in genetics,
it is now unclear whether Empress Dowager Cixi was aware that she had almost uprooted the foundations of the Qing dynasty,
leaving only Emperor Guangxu hidden in the cold palace,
and there being no suitable crown prince to learn and inherit the experience of Manchu rule over China.

1905 年中国停止了科举制度，
请注意，这发生在慈禧太后统治的晚年。
虽然后来的中国人民不承认，以文明的程度来说，
慈禧太后最后的几年却是中国史上进步最快的几年。
没有铁钳般的舆论钳制，没有报禁，没有党禁，
地方选举首次出现，而且朝廷甚至计划起草宪法。

In 1905, China abolished the imperial examination system.
Note that this occurred during the late years of Empress Dowager Cixi's rule.
Although later Chinese people did not recognize it, in terms of civilization,
the last few years of Empress Dowager Cixi were among the fastest progressive periods in Chinese history.
Without the iron-fisted control of public opinion, there was no press ban or party ban,
local elections appeared for the first time, and even the court planned to draft a constitution.

可惜，三年后 慈禧 死在 光绪 一天之后， 
有人说她一定要看到光绪死掉后她才肯撒手，
中国人喜爱的阴谋论不值得我们探索。仅从时间上来说，
这一个次序让 满清 丧失了顺利继承权力的条件。

Unfortunately, three years later, Cixi died a day after Guangxu. 
Some say she would not have let go until she saw Guangxu die,
but Chinese conspiracy theories are not worth exploring. Just from the timing,
this sequence deprived the Qing dynasty of the opportunity for a smooth power transition.

清朝究竟是不是殖民政权的问题长期以来是中国史学中的一个禁忌，
中国史研究中如果处处有禁忌的话，当然就无法被当做科学科目了。

The question of whether the Qing dynasty was a colonial regime has long been a taboo in Chinese historiography. If there are numerous taboos in the study of Chinese history, it naturally cannot be considered a scientific subject.

慈禧之后中国的统治者们也通常不承认清朝最后十年是更为文明的十年，
没有战争，愚民大法也失去了科举阵地，
统治者很低调... 但是
这些都无法阻止后世几代的领袖们把各种黑锅甩过来。

After Cixi, the rulers of China usually did not acknowledge that the final decade of the Qing Dynasty was a more civilized one, free from wars, and with the loss of the imperial examination system due to the suppression of the masses. The rulers were quite低调... But these facts could not prevent later generations of leaders from shoving all sorts of blame onto this period.

慈禧作为统治者的表现如果仅从八国联军之后到光绪死前这一段来看的话，
她是二十世纪最佳中国领导人，
几乎不用「之一」这样的后缀，
袁世凯、北洋军阀、孙中山、蒋介石、国民党、共产党， 都相差甚远，
其中孙中山的国民党 更是为中国人民 引入了 俄爹的狗崽子中共。

The ignorant cannot see the world of thought.
Learning to conquer others is the highest thought for savages.

在相对论提出时，清朝 并非没有 通讯设备和欧洲等地沟通，
朝庭的帝国电报管理局早在1881年就成立了，
此时已经发展到有数万公里的电报线路，
因此当时人们可以得到必要的资讯。
当然普通人一般不需要研究这些前沿的题目，
总的来说，专制体制对智慧的也摧残也并不缺这一个例子。 
与中国不同，日本学者则较快的开始研究相关的理论。

When the theory of relativity was proposed, the Qing Dynasty did not lack communication equipment to communicate with Europe and other places.
The Imperial Post Telegraph Bureau under the court had been established as early as 1881,
by which time it already had several tens of thousands of kilometers of telegraph lines.
Therefore, people could obtain necessary information at that time.
Of course, ordinary people generally did not need to study such cutting-edge topics.
In general, there are indeed many examples where authoritarian systems have also suppressed wisdom.
Unlike China, Japanese scholars began studying the related theories much more quickly.

日本并不是如同中国一般的专制，
在明治维新之后，
日本采用了立宪制，
并且开始积极的向西方学习，
日本对条约的尊重远胜中国，
他们是采用谈判的方式解决问题，
而不是中国式的武力对抗。

We must also mention that theology faces this same question: Is God within time?

在明治维新之前，日本亦没有堕落到中国的秦制集权。
以之前统治二百年的德川幕府为例，
它施行的是「幕藩制」。藩 就是「大名」的封地，
全国有 270 个藩，
大名在自己的藩里有权收税、司法、行政、军事，
而且幕府没有削藩，也就是说日本保持了真实的封建制。
在众多的大名中，外样大名 甚至是德川幕府以前的竞争者。

Before the Meiji Restoration, Japan had not sunk into the centralized autocracy of China's Qin Dynasty.
Taking the Tokugawa Shogunate, which ruled for two centuries, as an example,
it implemented the "Han System." A han was the fiefdom of a daimyo,
and there were 270 hans nationwide.
The daimyo had the authority to collect taxes, administer justice and governance, and maintain military power within their own han,
and the shogunate did not reduce the power of these han, meaning Japan maintained an actual feudal system.
Among many daimyos, the gosaihan (outer vassals) were even competitors of the Tokugawa Shogunate before.

至于中国，把自己的旧社会称为封建社会是因为搞错了封建的定义。
剥夺封地的「削藩」在中国历史早期就几乎是必备曲目了。
中国在秦朝以后并不是它的历史书中所谓的封建制度，
而是秦制，是中央集权，地方官员是中央任命的。
或者说中国在周朝之后就不再是封建了，
而是中央集权的农奴制 - 秦制。

As for China, referring to its old society as a feudal society is due to a misunderstanding of the definition of feudalism. 
The practice of depriving fiefs through "削藩" (削减诸侯权力) was almost a必备曲目 (required item) in early Chinese history.
After the Qin Dynasty, China was not the feudal system described in its historical books,
but rather the Qin System, centralization, with local officials appointed by the center.
Or one could say that after the Zhou Dynasty, China was no longer feudal,
but a form of serfdom under centralization - the Qin System.

日本社会被幕府区分为四个阶级：士农工商。
武士即属于：士。 这样的区分尽管古板僵硬，
却也没有中国这样的「万般皆下品，唯有读书高」的用科举毁灭思想毁灭技术毁灭一切。
-- 以刘仲敬-姨学-的语言来说：日本社会武德充沛。
-- 我们亦可以模仿的说：科举消灭武德。

Japanese society was divided into four classes under the feudal system: samurai, peasants, artisans, and merchants.
The samurai belonged to the "scholars" category. Although this classification was rigid,
it did not have the kind of "all is beneath; only reading is exalted" that China had, which used the imperial examination system to destroy ideas and technology and everything else.
-- In Liu Zhongjing's terminology: Japanese society was full of bushido (the way of the warrior).
-- We could also say in imitation: the imperial examination system extinguished bushido.

在更长时期来看，
日本则始终是幕府和天皇共存，
天皇在名义上最高，却没有实际权力，幕府执政。
这是实权和虚位并存，勉强算是一种权力制衡，
是一种分权。

From a longer perspective,
Japan persisted with the coexistence of the shogunate and the emperor,
with the emperor holding the nominal highest position but without actual power, while the shogunate held governance.
This was a coexistence of real power and mere formality,勉强算是一种权力制衡，
which could be considered a form of checks and balances.

简单的说，日本的制度体现了他们保守了道德底线。
在结构上，日本社会金字塔并没有彻底的归于唯一顶点。
在最高处有天皇 和 幕府大将军 并列，
在各地则有两百多大名，
甚至有很可能敌对的 「外样大名」。

To put it simply, Japan's system reflects that they have upheld moral底线.
Structurally, the Japanese society's pyramid is not entirely dominated by a single apex.
At the highest level, there are both the Emperor and the Shogun in parallel,
and at various regions, there are over two hundred daimyo,
even possibly including敌对的「外样大名」.

中国人常常认为日本是蛮夷小鬼子，
却没有注意到日本的社会制度是比宋元明清更优秀，
明治维新之后就更无法相提并论了。

Chinese people often regard Japan as barbarian little devils,
but they fail to notice that Japan's social system is superior to that of the Song, Yuan, Ming, and Qing dynasties.
After the Meiji Restoration, it is even more incomparable.

贪婪毁灭了道德，在社会结构上就直接体现出来了，
堕落的人们给自己修造了金字塔，爬在极权的台阶上沦为爬虫。
而爬虫们还自豪的认为自己的体制最伟大。

The greed destroyed morality, and this was directly reflected in the social structure,
Degenerate people built a pyramid for themselves, climbing the steps of tyranny and becoming vermin.
And these vermin are proud, believing that their system is the greatest.

金字塔力学的两种力量是暴力和谎言，
它的作用结果也是两方面的：摧毁人类的智慧和道德。

The two forces of pyramids mechanics are violence and lies,
and its resultant effects are dual-sided: destroying human wisdom and morality.

不论是观察中国史，还是对比日本社会，
金字塔的结构力量和它腐蚀人性的结果都很清楚明显。

Whether observing Chinese history or comparing Japanese society,
the power of the pyramid structure and its corrupting effect on human nature are clear and obvious.

日本甚至在几年之后就出现了一小段「大正民主」，
多党参政、妇女参政，自由主义，无政府主义，
社会主义都在日本社会出现了。
虽然很快转入军国主义，
由日本史上更强悍的武士们尝试解决问题。

If God does not roll a die,
can it still be said that God is omnipotent?

中日俄在东亚的博弈，
在之后的一个世纪里最终是以各自的德与智选择了各自的命运。

The rivalry among China, Japan, and Russia in East Asia,
ultimately chose their own destinies over the course of a century based on their respective virtues and wisdom.

文明的思想 想要进入中国的集权的金字塔 似乎须要穿透千年的重重的迷障。
而当文明的力量凭借着慈禧的 无知、功利、贪权和实用主义取得一些进展后，
又迅速地被后来的几代所谓伟人们以正义和进步的名义再次长久的扼杀了。

We certainly stayed too long in 1905.
However, the issue of the dice needs to be addressed.

在欧美学术界开始惊奇的认同相对论的这几年里，
垂死的大清朝正面对着崛起的大日本帝国。
但是，这是中国史上社会相对自由的一小段时间，
在清末这几年里，人们无须顾及朝庭的面子，

During the years when the Western academic world began to surprisingly acknowledge relativity,
the moribund Qing Dynasty faced the rising Empire of Japan.
However, this was a small period of relative social freedom in Chinese history.
In the final years of the Qing Dynasty, people did not need to worry about the court's face,

我们也许在1905年停留的太久了。

We might have stayed in 1905 too long.

五亿大清子民，是五亿条落入集权金字塔陷阱中的血脉。

Half a billion citizens of the Qing Dynasty are half a billion veins ensnared in the trap of centralized power.

如果血脉并没有传递思想，
那么也许思想就像是漂在时空中的花粉，

If the bloodline does not transmit thoughts,

Ignorant people cannot see the world of ideas,

After the theory of relativity was proposed, it was found that time begins to slow down as speed approaches the speed of light. Temporarily setting aside the question of whether photons are stationary on the timeline, philosophy immediately had no choice but to return to face an ancient problem: what is time?

我们需要额外指出神学也同样要面对这个问题：神也在时间之内吗？

We also need to point out that theology faces this issue as well: Does God exist within time?

如果时间不是古人们想像的样子， 一切都要从新理解。 
人们因此开始了解时间和空间的规律。术业有专攻，
作者就不想轻易的乱讲更具体的学术内容了，
有兴趣的读者请自行搜索。
比如，光速在时间中扮演了什么角色，
从 电磁波的麦克斯韦方程能不能推导出光速，等等。

For example, if someone becomes a dice in God’s hands,
can God predict his actions?

在二十世纪之初，慈禧太后 有幸和 爱因斯坦 共存的这段时空中，
想想， 这一段时间距离我们似乎并不遥远，甚至是太近了。

In the early twentieth century, during the coexisting timeline with Empress Dowager Cixi and Einstein,
consider that this period of time seems not so distant from us, almost too close.
As for Jane, her famous saying is: Time is a knife!

可是我的天啊，
我不仅不是这个意思，而且如刀的绝不是岁月，
残忍地斩去生命、思想和良知的绝不是岁月。
至少在中国，斩去人类最宝贵的一切的，
剥夺人性与文明的东西是来自地狱的几何武器：集权的黑三角。

Note: Emperor Guangxu and Empress Dowager Cixi died that year.

岁月不属于这个黑三角，时间的维度是它无法包容的，
低维权力空间没有包容时间维度，二千年的历史说明，
它没有随着岁月变化，它始终都是愚民暴政。
对它说什么岁月，真是侮辱了岁月。

At the time, Ford's cars amazed people,
being magical and useful with affordable prices.

下一代的愚民们也看不到这一个三角形，但是，
是什么斩去了人们的最基本的智力？就是它，
那些成功的正义的爬了上去的伟人和它们的同僚同志们只是身陷其中而不自知的爬虫类，无有例外。
清末人口5.2亿，三角形面积 5.2亿，
这就是中国人生存的世界，爬向顶点的就是伟人了。
五亿条血脉长久的陷落在这一个降维度的空间里，
打破空间壁垒是不可能的，
记住爬虫们看不到这个伟大的三角形。

The next generation of the ill-informed also fail to see this triangle, but,
What has cut off people's basic intelligence? It is precisely them—the great and just individuals who succeeded and climbed up, along with their fellow comrades—who are oblivious to their own nature as worms.
At the end of the Qing Dynasty, there were 520 million people; the area of the triangle was 5.2 billion,
This is the world in which Chinese people survive; those who climb to the top are the great ones.
For a long time, fifty billion bloodlines have been trapped in this lower-dimensional space,
Breaking through the spatial barriers is impossible.
Remember that the worms cannot see this magnificent triangle.

回到 1905 年，那段岁月是物理学的黄金岁月。
爱因斯坦和其它物理学家同时也帮助人们看到了另一个世界 ：
微观的量子世界。 在微观世界， 连续性中断了，
能量在极小但是不连续的台阶上跳着玩耍。

Return to 1905, those years were the golden age of physics.
Together with other physicists, Einstein also helped people glimpse another world:
the microscopic quantum world. In this microscopic world, continuity was interrupted,
energy played on very small but discontinuous steps.

牛顿力学让人们保持因果律的思路， F=MA, 
经典力学符合多数中国人的潜意识：力量是原因，努力就可以改变命运。 
而量子力学中却普遍出现了几率的概念。

Newtonian mechanics keeps people thinking in terms of causality, with F=MA. 
Classical mechanics aligns with the subconscious thoughts of most Chinese people: force is the cause, and effort can change one's fate.
In quantum mechanics, however, the concept of probability is widely prevalent.

几率和概率是一个东西，
从概率的本质来说， 
如果上帝要投骰子，
如果我们假设上帝全能全知， 
他应该可以让自己的骰子完全随机。

The odds and probability are the same thing.
From the essence of probability,
if God were to roll a die,
assuming God is omniscient and omnipotent,
He should be able to make His dice roll completely random.

可是， 如果上帝不知道下一个骰子抛出来是几点，

But if God does not know what number will come up on the next dice roll,

如果说上帝不抛骰子，

If God does not throw dice,

「上帝不抛骰子」这是爱因斯坦说的。

"Einstein once said, 'God does not play dice.'"
